About Fit Freak

A fitness centre built around how you actually train.

Located in Askari V, Malir Cantonment, Fit Freak Gym & Fitness Centre brings strength training, cardio conditioning and structured group classes into one facility — designed for the physical fitness and healthy lifestyle goals of individuals and families alike.

Whether you're starting out on the gym floor, working one-on-one with a coach, or joining a scheduled group class, every session at Fit Freak is built around a member-first experience: proper guidance, consistent scheduling, and a training environment that keeps you coming back.

Inside The Gym

The floor, the focus, the work.

Questions

Everything before your first session.

Registration is a one-time fee of Rs. 5,000, charged in addition to whichever membership term and track you choose.

Ladies sessions run Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

Gents sessions run Monday to Saturday, 6:00 AM to 10:00 AM and again 6:00 PM to 12:00 AM.

The gym is closed on Sundays and public holidays.

Personal Training (Rs. 15,000) is dedicated one-on-one coaching. Target Training (Rs. 25,000) is more intensive, goal-specific programming for a defined outcome.

Rs. 16,000 per month covers Strength, Cardio, Zumba, Aerobics and Yoga, scheduled across 5 days a week.

Members under 23 have a dedicated quarterly rate: Rs. 5,000 Strength, Rs. 4,000 Cardio, or Rs. 7,000 Full Access.

Yes — Generic Floor Assistance for form and machine guidance is free for all members.
